Go anywhere on the field

The whole field is generated from plant data, so what you see is what the model knows: tagged, connected and open to inspect.

The plant is running

Behind the model is a dynamic simulation of the wells, separation, compression, export, power and utilities. The control room console is where you operate it.

When something goes wrong

One upset, followed through the twin from the first detector to the investigation.

  1. A flange starts to leak

    It lets go at the HP separator outlet, and one gas detector sees it.

  2. A second detector agrees

    The console puts the camera up on its own. The camera detections are simulated too.

  3. The platform shuts itself down

    Emergency shutdown. The zone blows down to the flare.

  4. The gas ignites

    The deluge comes on and the fire pumps start.

  5. An agent works out what happened

    Every step it takes is on the record. In this demo it is a simulated local planner, not a live model.

Rehearse it before it happens

The same plant is a training ground, so a crew can practise a bad day on a quiet one.

Bring your own plant
