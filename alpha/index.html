Dynamic process simulation

Wells, separation, compression, export, power and utilities run in a dynamic simulation, operated from the control room console.

Emergency response

Follow one incident from the first gas alarm to the final investigation.

  1. Gas leak detected

    A flange leaks at the high-pressure separator, and one gas detector raises an alarm.

  2. Second detector confirms

    The console displays the nearest camera automatically. SAM 3.1 segmentation identifies people in view and raises alerts in real time.

  3. Emergency shutdown

    The platform shuts down automatically, and gas from the affected area is routed to the flare.

  4. Fire response

    The gas ignites. The deluge system activates and the fire pumps start.

  5. Incident investigation

    An agent reconstructs the event and records every step. In this demo it is a simulated local planner, not a live AI model.
