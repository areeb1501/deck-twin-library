When something goes wrong

Follow one incident from the first gas alarm through to the investigation.

  1. A flange starts to leak

    Gas escapes at the high-pressure separator, and one detector picks it up.

  2. A second detector confirms it

    The console brings up the nearest camera on its own. SAM 3.1 segmentation identifies people on screen and raises alerts in real time.

  3. The platform shuts itself down

    An emergency shutdown starts, and gas from the affected area is sent to the flare.

  4. The gas ignites

    The deluge system sprays the area with water and the fire pumps start.

  5. An agent investigates

    It works out what happened and records every step. In this demo it is a simulated local planner, not a live AI model.
